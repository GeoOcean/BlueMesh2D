description=Generate unstructured triangular meshes from bathymetry rasters (BlueMesh2D)
about=Wraps the BlueMesh2D meshing library as a QGIS Processing algorithm. Extracts a coastline/domain from a bathymetry GeoTIFF, builds a depth-based, gradient-limited element-size function (with an optional local detail region), refines and smooths a triangular mesh, samples bathymetry onto the nodes, and writes a UGRID NetCDF that loads as a QGIS mesh layer. Requires numpy, scipy, shapely, rasterio, pyproj, xarray, netCDF4 and triangle in the QGIS Python environment.
